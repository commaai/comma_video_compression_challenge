This submission encodes the source HEVC footage with SVT-AV1 after downscaling to 45 percent of the original resolution using a Lanczos filter, then upscales back to the native 1164x874 resolution at decode time with bicubic interpolation and unsharp masking. The approach exploits the fact that the SegNet and PoseNet evaluators respond primarily to road geometry and scene structure in the forward-facing view rather than to pixel-level texture, so aggressive spatial downscaling incurs negligible evaluator distortion while substantially reducing the compressed bitrate.
A grid search was run over SCALE in {0.40, 0.45, 0.50} and CRF in {30, 33, 36}, nine configurations in total, with SVT-AV1 preset 0, film grain synthesis level 22, and a 180-frame keyframe interval. SCALE=0.45 with CRF=33 achieved the lowest composite score of 2.09, computed as 100 times SegNet distortion 0.00559 plus the square root of 10 times PoseNet distortion 0.08382 plus 25 times compression rate 0.02443. This compresses the 37.5 MB original to a 917,395 byte archive. Scores were confirmed on CPU to match the public leaderboard evaluation environment.
