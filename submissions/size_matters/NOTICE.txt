Size Matters builds on darskkaa's public submission #151:
https://github.com/commaai/comma_video_compression_challenge/pull/151

The inherited decoder is in vendor/ and is unchanged. Its preceding work
includes submissions #150, #146, #141, #140, #135 and #130.
The inherited HPAC rowpack layout also credits Bubu631's #148 work:
https://github.com/commaai/comma_video_compression_challenge/pull/148 The license
notices are retained in LICENSE and vendor/external/pr141/LICENSE.

The added encoder recodes the model's integer arrays and restores the
same weights, renderer representation and coded video data for that decoder.

The carrier codec predicts RGB basis planes with stored integer coefficients.
The trailer codec restores the original residual table, warp choices and
correction records. All fitted predictors and coding settings are stored
in the archive; no video data or model weights are embedded in the source.
